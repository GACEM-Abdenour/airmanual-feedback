# AeroMind Feedback Dashboard - User Guide

Welcome to the **AeroMind Feedback Dashboard**. This interface is designed to help you organize, structure, and refine the knowledge base to ensure the highest quality of AI responses. 

By creating explicit expected answers and safety redlines, you are actively training the system to meet your exact standards.

---

## 1. Managing Questions

The primary workflow revolves around creating explicit examples of how questions should be answered.

* **Creating a Question:** Click the **Add New Question** button at the top right. An empty card will appear where you can enter the Question, Expected Response, Key Points, and Required Resources. The text boxes will automatically expand as you type. Click "Save" when finished.
* **Editing Content:** To modify an existing entry, click the **Pencil Icon** (✏️) on the top right of any question card. Adjust the text directly inline, and hit "Update Question".
* **Deleting:** Click the **Trash Can Icon** (🗑️) to permanently remove a question. You will be asked to confirm.
* **Quick Copying:** If you need to extract text quickly, hover over any text box (like "Response" or "Key Points") to reveal a **Copy Icon**. Clicking this will instantly copy the contents to your clipboard.

## 2. Collaboration & Feedback

The dashboard allows your team to evaluate and discuss specific question formats.

* **Reactions:** Use the **Thumbs Up** (👍) or **Thumbs Down** (👎) buttons at the bottom of a question card to signify if the current expected answer is accurate or needs improvement.
* **Comments:** Leave notes or discuss edge-cases with your team using the **Comments** section at the bottom of each card.

## 3. Global Rules & Categories

To ensure consistency, you can set overarching rules that apply to the entire system or specific topics.

* **Global Rules (Category 0):** Navigate to the **Global Rules** tab on the left menu. Here you can define systemic instructions (e.g., "Always prioritize safety warnings") and strict Redlines (e.g., "Never suggest bypassing a physical inspection").
* **Categories:** On the same tab, you can create specific tags (e.g., *Tail Rotor Systems*). Once created, you can assign these tags to questions. Each category can also have its own unique set of rules and redlines that only apply to that topic.

## 4. Excel Import & Export

You can easily backup or batch-edit your entire database using Excel.

* **Export Excel:** Click the **Export Excel** button to download a complete `.xlsx` snapshot of your database. The file will contain two separate sheets: one for your Questions, and one for your Categories/Global Rules.
* **Import Excel:** If you prefer to write questions in Excel, you can use the downloaded file as a template. Make your changes in Excel, click **Import Excel** in the dashboard, and select your file. The dashboard will instantly update to match your spreadsheet!
